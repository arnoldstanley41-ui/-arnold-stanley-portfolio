About Me

Law, Institutions, and the Changing World They Govern.

I am a lawyer and legal researcher based in Bengaluru, interested in how legal institutions respond to technological, geopolitical, and social change. My work is shaped by a broader concern with the way law distributes authority, structures governance, resolves disputes, and adapts when established doctrines encounter unfamiliar realities. This has drawn me to questions across international law, dispute resolution, public policy, institutional design, and emerging legal challenges.

My research combines doctrinal analysis with an interest in practical legal reform. Across different projects, I have examined digital infrastructure and sovereignty, changing conceptions of jurisdiction, arbitration, environmental governance, artificial intelligence, intellectual property, and regulatory decision-making. Although these subjects differ, they are connected by a common inquiry: how can legal frameworks remain principled and legitimate while responding effectively to new forms of power, risk, and institutional complexity?

Alongside my academic work, I have developed experience through legal internships, fellowship programmes, peer-review and editorial responsibilities, competitions, and research presentations before academic audiences in India and abroad. These experiences have strengthened my commitment to careful research, clear writing, and intellectual independence. I aim to produce work that connects rigorous legal reasoning with the practical realities faced by courts, regulators, institutions, and those affected by their decisions.
